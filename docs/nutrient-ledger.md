# Nutrient and material ledger

The annual ledger tracks nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium as stocks and
flows. For each year and nutrient it enforces:

`opening stock + external inputs + biological additions = closing stock + exports + losses`

Crop residues, leaf fall, root turnover, pruning, compost, manure, bedding,
food waste and treated human excreta are internal transfers. They move material
between pools but do not create new fertility. Harvested food, seed, trade,
livestock products, volatilization, leaching, erosion and mortality are
separate outputs or losses.

Humanure is an optional scenario with urine and faecal pathways. Recovery,
treatment, storage, volatilization and crop-availability factors are explicit;
only treated available nutrients are credited. Approval, pathogen and
contaminant controls remain site-specific and are not implied by the ledger.

Fixing plants distinguish nitrogen fixed from nitrogen transferred to crops.
Black locust uses the documented 30/43/56 kg N/ha/year sensitivity. Honey
locust uses a replaceable black-locust proxy at 25%/50%/75%, producing
7.5/21.5/42 kg N/ha/year. The proxy is a planning assumption, not a local
measurement. The same distinction applies to annual legumes, peashrub and
autumn olive.

Imported feed energy and protein remain zero in canonical ARC livestock
scenarios. Mineral, veterinary and nutrient supplements are disclosed as
external non-food inputs rather than misclassified as biological feed.
