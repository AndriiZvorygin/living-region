# Mature recurring-labour audit

The canonical plants-only mature estimate includes annual cultivation, perennial food management and woody heating work. It excludes one-time establishment labour. Hours are planning assumptions, not a Grey-Bruce time-and-motion study.

## Ordinary one-adult activity audit

| activity | hours/year | labour class |
|---|---:|---|

The resulting ordinary one-adult totals are 7.8 heavy annual-cultivation hours, 24.3 heavy woody hours, 32.2 physically demanding hours overall, 75.5 light/moderate recurring hours, 26.5 processing/storage hours and 134.2 total recurring hours/year. The ageing metric is the 70% reduction in annual cultivation area; it does not imply that total work falls by the same percentage.
